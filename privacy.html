Privacy Policy

Salca Upload Tool is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data the app uses

  • Through Google sign-in (OAuth), the app gets permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel and to read that channel's list of videos (titles and publishing dates).
  • It uses this only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to avoid repeating titles or scheduling two videos on the same day.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens and logs are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.
